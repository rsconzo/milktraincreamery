✦ Our Story

Small business.
Big scoops.

Milk Train Creamery was born in 2025 with one simple goal: bring seriously good ice cream to Clover, SC. We're a family-owned shop that puts quality and community first.

From classic sundaes topped sky-high to shakes that'll stop you in your tracks — everything we make is crafted with care and served with a smile. Come as you are, stay as long as you like.
